I invite you to take part in the different cities where FLISOL will be held in Venezuela and in the various countries of Latin America. The event will take place tomorrow, Saturday April 9. For more information about Flisol in Venezuela go [here (broken link)](http://flisol.org.ve/) or for Latin America [here](http://flisol.net/FLISOL2011).


In my case I will take part in Flisol in the city of Cabudare in Lara State, at the Universidad de Yacambu.

![FLISOL Cabure](./images/flisol_Cabudare.jpg)

The talk I will give tomorrow at 2pm is about Programming on Android with Python.
