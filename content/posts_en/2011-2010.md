The year that has just ended had its joys and sorrows, and the most important is that my father is no longer with us.

As for the blog, I almost matched the number of articles written in 2009, falling short by just one.

I had promised myself to write 48 articles but, due to various circumstances, I could not meet that goal. Well, I will try to meet it this year. Besides writing in Spanish, I will start writing in English on my other blog so that this one is only for articles in Spanish; so I will have to translate the old articles that are still up to date and update and translate the ones that are not.

I will keep writing about android and python, and I will finish the series of articles on python+gtk+glade so I can start writing about pyqt. I will also find the time to write about Django, keep writing about Debian packaging aimed at python, and some things about network administration and security with Linux.

Now on to the blog summary:

* In 2010 I wrote 35 articles (the 36th was going to be this article).
* The month when the most people visited the blog was December, with 1721 visits.
* The most read article was python on Android with 665 visits, followed by basic adb(Android) commands with 317 visits and in third place Developing graphical applications with python+gtk+glade with 235 visits.
* The blog has 3 important references: the python wiki and the wiki of the SL4A tutorials hosted on google code.

Well, during the month I will start the technical articles. I send a happy new year to everyone who follows me, thank you for doing so!!!!.
