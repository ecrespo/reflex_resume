As explained on [wikipedia](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_de_las_cosas), the internet of things is a concept that refers to the interconnection of everyday objects to the Internet.

A while ago I was asked to research the topic and I made a presentation. Since I never had the chance to give a formal presentation on it, I have decided to publish it now, stripped of the format of the site where they asked me for that research.

You can see the presentation on [slideshare](https://www.slideshare.net/ecrespo/internet-de-las-cosas-63892329).
