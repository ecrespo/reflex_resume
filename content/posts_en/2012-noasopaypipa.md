Today an article I wrote was published in the newspaper [Ciudad Caracas](http://www.ciudadccs.info/?p=250335) . In it I summarize what SOPA and PIPA are and how they would affect all of us if they are approved.

I will present three videos that explain in more detail what SOPA and PIPA are and how they will affect us if these bills are approved.

For that reason the Debian Venezuela site will have a protest page, as the figure shows:

![Texto alternativo](./images/noasopaypipa-3.png)

This blog will be in protest tomorrow from 8am until 8am the next day, as the figure shows:

![Texto alternativo](./images/noasopaypipa-4.png)

And why this? To prevent many sites from ending up with a message saying they are censored:

![Texto alternativo](./images/noasopaypipa-5.png)

So far the sites that will be protesting are:

- Wikipedia
- Identi.ca
- Mozilla
- FreeSoftware Fundation
- Reddit
- Tucows
- XDA Developers 
- Cuevana
- Anonymous
- Diaspora
- Twicpic
- Wordpress

And the list keeps growing; if you want to see the complete list you can find it [here](http://nlb-creations.com/2012/01/13/websites-confirmed-for-the-jan-18-blackout-protesting-sopa/).

From Latin America, Hipatia, Solar, Solve Colibris, Debian-ve, etc. are joining.

Those who want to support the protest by placing banners, or who want an infographic, can find them at the following links:

- Infographic: You can find it on the [Derecho a Leer](http://derechoaleer.org/blog/) page.
- Protest template for your sites: you can find it in the article by [alt1040 (broken link)](https://hipertextual.com/archivo/2012/01/sopa-plantilla-para-sumar-tu-sitio-web-a-la-protesta-del-18j/).
- If your sites or blogs use Drupal, Wordpress or Blogger, there is a guide on how to protest on the [Cibernauta Latino](https://ciberlatino.wordpress.com/2012/01/16/sopa-pipa-como-poner-tu-sitio-web-en-modo-blackout/) site.
