I have gone several days without writing anything on the blog; first there is the local situation, and the other thing is that I am thinking of restructuring the blogs I have.

The blogs I have are:

- [ernestocrespo.blogspot.com](http://ernestocrespo.blogspot.com/): A blog in English that I have somewhat neglected. I plan to migrate this blog first to the url [http://blog2.crespo.org.ve (broken link)](http://blog2.crespo.org.ve) and I will use [pelican](http://github.com/getpelican/pelican) for the content.   
- [blog.crespo.org.ve](/blog): The Spanish version of the blog, which has more than 300 posts. Since this blog has the most content, the migration will be done in stages with a beta version named [blog3.crespo.org.ve (broken link)](http://blog3.crespo.org.ve/); when the migration is ready it will become [blog.crespo.org.ve](/blog).    
- [Medium (broken link)](http://medium.com/@_seraph1): This blog will be for general non-technical topics or a few technical ones (very few).     
- [ernestocrespo13.wordpress.com](http://ernestocrespo13.wordpress.com/): This is normally a backup of the Spanish blog; it will now hold the posts of both blogs, in English and Spanish, as a backup.

In the future I will stop publishing on blogspot and wordpress, leaving only the pelican blogs.

Another activity I have is to bring up [www.crespo.org.ve (broken link)](http://www.crespo.org.ve/), and have the two blogs together with the Medium one linked there.

The other point is that I am going to start using URL shorteners that allow monetizing the blog, just as I have already placed links to receive donations via Bitcoin wallet or paypal.
