Title: Debian Day in Mérida.
Date: 2011-08-27 10:00
Category: Evento
Tags: Dia Debian
lang: en
translation: true

Today Debian Day will be celebrated at Fundacite Mérida on the occasion of Debian's 18th birthday.

Place: Av. Alberto Carnevali via La Hechicera, behind the MINFRA, Building B of Fundacite Mérida. 

Additionally, since this week Linux turned 20 years old, we will celebrate that too.

Here is a video commemorating the date.

[20th anniversary of Linux - Subtitled in Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MTBX0j86Ca4)

The scheduled talks are the following:

* A video commemorating 20 years of Linux will be shown.
* Building a custom Debian distro. (Franklin G. Mendoza).
* [How to contribute to Debian](https://www.slideshare.net/ecrespo/colaborar-en-debian) (Ernesto Crespo). 
* Graphical environments in Debian (Marie Matos).
* Configuring Plone for High Availability on Debian production servers (Leonardo Caballero).
* OpenLDAP in Debian (Daniela Matos).
* Web servers for specific needs (Hector Colina).
* [Configuration management in Debian](https://www.slideshare.net/ecrespo/gestin-de-configuracin-con-mercurial-y-etckeeper) (Ernesto Crespo).
